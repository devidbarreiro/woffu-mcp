Clock in from
your terminal

MCP server for Woffu. Connect it to Claude, Cursor, Windsurf, or any AI agent. Say "fichame" and you're clocked in.

$ npx woffu-mcp click to copy

7 tools. One command.

clock_toggle Auto-detects entry or exit. The one you'll use every day.
clock_in Force clock in. Skips if already clocked in.
clock_out Force clock out. Skips if not clocked in.
get_status Working or not, today's signs, schedule, vacation balance.
get_week_summary Hours worked vs scheduled this week, per day.
get_vacations Allocated, used, available. All your days at a glance.
get_holidays Company holidays for any year. Never miss a day off.

How it works

Your agent talks to Woffu through MCP. No middleware, no hosted service. Everything runs on your machine.

Your Agent → woffu-mcp → Woffu API

Clock logic is automatic. Even sign count means entry, odd means exit. Holidays and weekends are skipped. Your credentials never leave your machine.

What is MCP?

Model Context Protocol is an open standard by Anthropic that lets AI agents connect to external tools. Think USB-C for AI: one protocol, many tools. Any agent that supports MCP can use this server.

What is Woffu?

Woffu is an HR platform used by thousands of companies in Spain and Latin America for time tracking, attendance, and vacation management. Required by Spanish labor law (Real Decreto-ley 8/2019).
